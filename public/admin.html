WAREHOUSE GUIDE
F
01 · Near entrance

Front display

Signed copies, first editions, rare finds and special items chosen for prominent display. Premium pricing requires partner judgment.

Special / premium
M
02 · Retail floor

Middle retail

Routine modern, mass-produced books after the initial exception screen. Verified MSRP can be the baseline; discounts are intentional decisions.

Junior staff allowed
A
03 · Separate department

Annex retail

Coffee, snacks, beverages, gifts, stationery and other merchandise. Use quantity tracking and supplier / reorder details as those fields are added.

Merchandise
R
04 · Behind the desk

Restricted backroom

Old, antique, special, unpriced, hard-to-value or backlog books. No public display or junior-staff discount without partner approval.

Partner review only
✓Modern is not automatically ordinary. Signed, first-edition or otherwise special books must be flagged and reviewed even if they have a barcode.
✓Zone and channel are different. A book can be on the Front shelf but still unpublished online until a partner approves its listing.
✓Every copy has its own identity. Keep condition, price and location at the individual-copy level for unique stock.
