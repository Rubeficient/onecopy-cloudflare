A little about us

Good books never really leave us.

Magic Door Books is an independent bookstore on Second Street in Pomona. We believe a book is more than a temporary commodity: every copy carries a history, every reader brings a new one, and the right book can find its person years after it was printed.

We’re carefully building our online catalog while keeping the pleasure of discovery at the heart of the shop.
